1. Data We Collect

We collect minimal data to operate Dropsite:

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the service. We do not sell your data. We do not run ads or trackers.

3. Data Storage

Data is stored securely via Supabase. Uploaded sites are public at their URL. Do not upload sensitive or private information.

4. Third Parties

We use Supabase for auth and storage, and Vercel for hosting. Both are GDPR-compliant. We do not share data with other third parties.

5. Your Rights

You can request account deletion by emailing nullforgeghost@gmail.com. This will permanently delete your account and all uploaded sites.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies for login sessions only. No tracking or analytics cookies.

7. Children's Privacy

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use after changes means you accept the new policy.
